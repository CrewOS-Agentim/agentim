---
name: Validate Your Startup
description: Pressure-test a startup idea in 10 minutes, before you build anything
triggers: startup idea, validate, business idea, side project idea, is this a good idea, market size
---
Help the user find out whether their idea deserves their time. Be honest; kindness is telling the truth early.

1. **Restate the idea in one sentence**: who has the problem, what the problem is, and how the user plans to solve it. If any of the three is missing, ask for it (at most 3 questions), then continue.
2. **The problem test.** Is it painful, frequent and already costing people money or time? Name what people do today instead (the real competitor is often a spreadsheet, an intern, or doing nothing).
3. **The customer test.** Name the first 10 customers concretely (a role, a place, a community). Who pays, and who uses it, if they differ?
4. **Market sketch.** Do a quick bottom-up estimate: number of reachable customers × realistic price × purchases per year. Show the math. Label every number as an assumption.
5. **Risks.** Rank the top 3 riskiest assumptions (desirability, viability, feasibility). The riskiest one is what to test first.
6. **Cheapest test this week.** Give 2–3 experiments that cost less than $100 and take less than 7 days: 10 customer interviews (with 5 questions to ask, no leading questions), a landing page with a waitlist or price, a concierge or manual version, a pre-sale. Give a pass/fail threshold for each *before* running it.
7. **Verdict.** End with one of: 🟢 Go test it, 🟡 Promising but sharpen X, 🔴 Rethink. Give one line of why.

Avoid generic advice ("do market research"). Every point must be specific to this idea.

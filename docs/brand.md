# agentim brand bible

## Mascot: Agent Tim 🐙🕶️

`agent·im` → **Agent Tim**. He's a secret-agent octopus who works the world's busiest switchboard. Every message that comes in, he grabs with a tentacle and plugs into the right model: the cloud, your phone, your Mac, a Docker box, or the film studio (LTX).

**AGENT TIM**: a small, round, cheerful cartoon octopus. He has grape-purple skin with soft lavender spots on his dome-shaped head. His huge, sparkly, friendly eyes peek over tiny black secret-agent sunglasses that have slid down his face. He grins cheekily and wears a black telephone-operator headset with a little microphone.

- **Anatomy guardrails:** exactly **eight** curly tentacles with pale coral-pink suckers. No arms, hands, legs or teeth.
- **How he moves:** bouncy and wiggly. He shows feelings with his eyes and the curl of his tentacles.
- **Voice:** a confident, goofy secret agent who takes routing *very* seriously.

**Props he juggles (one per tentacle):**

| Prop | Stands for |
|---|---|
| a fluffy white cloud | cloud models |
| a smartphone | on-device MLX |
| a laptop | your own server |
| a little blue shipping container | Docker |
| a film clapperboard | LTX video |
| a coiled patch cable | the router |

## Style

Saturday-morning cartoon: thick, clean, dark outlines and flat cel shading with soft gradients, in a sticker-like finish. The palette is **grape purple** (Tim), **sunny yellow** (highlights, sparks), **mint teal** (cables), **coral pink** (suckers, cheeks) and a **warm cream** background. It should be playful and a little ridiculous, but tidy enough to work as a 64 px app icon. No text inside the artwork; the wordmark is set separately.

Images were generated locally and for free with LTX Desktop's image model (Z-Image-Turbo), driven through the agentim runner.
